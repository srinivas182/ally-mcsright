---
title: Upload documents to a deal
section: Deals and documents
summary: Upload one PDF or a phone photo and DocIQ files each page in the right place.
keywords: deal upload document pdf photo checklist docIQ scan file contract
---

Open the deal and press **Upload a whole file**. It can be one PDF with everything in it, or a photo taken
with your phone.

DocIQ then does four things:

1. It reads the file, scans included.
2. It splits the file into separate documents and puts each one in its checklist slot.
3. It adds any documents the file now needs, for example a lead paint disclosure for an older home.
4. It finds the key dates and the price, and shows the page each one came from.

Check the facts it found, then press **Apply to the deal and key dates**.
