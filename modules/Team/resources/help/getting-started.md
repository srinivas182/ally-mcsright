The menu on the left is grouped by what you do: **Overview** (your day and your deals), **Grow** (leads,
contacts and marketing), **People**, and **Settings**.

Do these three things first:

1. **Add your photo and phone number** under Settings › Profile, so clients and your broker see them.
2. **Turn on two-step sign-in** under Settings › Security. It keeps your clients' information safe.
3. **Open "Today"**. It lists your calls, tasks and deadlines for the day, most urgent first.

Stuck? Search this help centre or press **Ask us** at the bottom of this page.
