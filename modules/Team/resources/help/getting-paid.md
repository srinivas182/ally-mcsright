---
title: How you get paid at closing
section: Money
summary: Where to see what you will be paid, and when.
keywords: commission pay payout split money closing cda 1099 earnings
---

Open **My money**. For each deal you see:

- the commission;
- your split and any fees;
- what you will be paid.

Each amount links to the plan it came from. When the deal closes and the money arrives, your broker approves the
payout and you get an email.

Keep your W-9 up to date in **My money**, so your brokerage can send your yearly tax form (1099) in January.
