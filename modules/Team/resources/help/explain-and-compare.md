Under each document on a deal you will find these options:

- **Explain in plain English** or **Explicar en español**. You get short points about money, dates and duties,
  each taken from a page of the document. It is not legal advice; the document itself is what counts.
- **What changed from the last version?** Shown when a new version replaces an old one, for example a counter
  offer. You see the price, the dates and the lines that were added or taken out.
- **Safe link.** Makes a link that works for 1, 3 or 7 days. It opens a copy with Social Security and bank
  numbers blacked out, for a lender or title company.
