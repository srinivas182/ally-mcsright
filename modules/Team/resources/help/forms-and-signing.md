---
title: Send forms for e-signature
section: Deals and documents
summary: Fill forms from the deal and send them to sign in a few clicks.
keywords: form sign esign signature packet addendum send buyer seller
---

On the deal, open **Forms** and pick the forms you need. The price, address and names fill in from the deal.

Press **Send to sign**. Each person gets a link by email. They can sign on a phone, and each person signs only
their own fields. You see who has signed, and the finished copy is filed in the deal by itself.
