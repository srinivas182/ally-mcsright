title: Turn your own PDF into a fillable form
section: Deals and documents
summary: For brokers - upload a PDF and let RightAlly find the blanks.
keywords: form builder pdf fields blanks template upload smart fillable
who: owner
---

Go to **Settings › Forms**, upload the PDF, open it and press **Find the fields for me**.

RightAlly finds the fill-in lines and tick boxes, scans included. It names each field from the words next to it,
and links it to the deal (price, address, names) or to the person who signs it.

Check each field in the editor, then save. Running it again never adds a field on top of one already there.
