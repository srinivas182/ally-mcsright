Open **Team chat** from the menu.

- **Announcements**: news from your broker. Everyone reads it; only the broker and staff post.
- **Everyone**: the whole office.
- **Groups**: an office, a team or a committee, set up by your broker.
- **Direct**: a private chat between you and one other person. Press **New direct message**.

Type **@** and pick a name to mention someone. If they have not read it within 10 minutes, they get an email.
You can change or take back your own message for 15 minutes.
