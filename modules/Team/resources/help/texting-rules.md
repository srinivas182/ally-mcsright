US law (the TCPA) protects people from unwanted texts and calls. RightAlly follows these rules for you:

- We text only people who agreed to texts. Their agreement is saved with the wording they saw.
- Texts and automatic calls go out only between 8 am and 9 pm in the person's own time zone.
- If someone replies **STOP**, they are never texted again. Numbers on the Do Not Call list are skipped.

If you are unsure, call the person yourself or send an email instead.
