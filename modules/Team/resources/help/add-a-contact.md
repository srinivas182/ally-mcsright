---
title: Add a contact or bring in your list
section: Leads and contacts
summary: Add one person by hand, or upload a spreadsheet of your past clients.
keywords: add contact import csv spreadsheet sphere past clients upload
---

**One person:** open **Contacts** and press **Add contact**. A name and a phone or email is enough.

**Your whole list:** open **Contacts** and choose **Import**, then upload a CSV file (most phones and email
programs can save one). Match each column to a field, and RightAlly skips anyone already there.

Permission to text is never imported. People must agree to texts themselves.
