section: Getting started

- **Turn on two-step sign-in** under Settings › Security.
- **RightAlly never asks for your password** by email, text or phone. Nor does our support team.
- **Wire fraud:** never send or forward wiring instructions by email. Always confirm them by phone, on a number
  you already know. RightAlly warns you when a document holds bank details.
