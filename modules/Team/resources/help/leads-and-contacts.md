Every new lead lands in **Leads**, whichever site it came from: your website, Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com,
Facebook lead ads or a form you set up.

- You get an alert by text or email when a lead is yours.
- Press **Call** to be connected. Leads called within five minutes are far more likely to talk to you.
- After the call, choose what happened. RightAlly sets the next task for you.

We only text people who agreed to texts, and only between 8 am and 9 pm their time.
