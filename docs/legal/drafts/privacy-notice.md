# DRAFT: RightAlly privacy notice (for attorney review)

_Plain-language draft. Not for publication until reviewed._

## Who we are

RightAlly provides software that real estate brokerages use to run their business. For the information a brokerage
keeps about its clients, **the brokerage is in charge** and RightAlly works on its behalf. For people who sign up to
RightAlly itself, and for visitors to rightally.app, RightAlly is in charge.

## What we keep

- **Brokerage staff and agents:** name, email, phone, sign-in and security details, licence details, the work they do
  in RightAlly, and their pay records where the brokerage uses RightAlly for commissions.
- **Brokerages' clients and leads:** what the brokerage or agent records — name, contact details, homes they are
  interested in, notes, messages, documents and deal details — and what they send through the brokerage's website.
- **Website visitors:** pages visited and, if they agree, how they found the site; enquiries they send.

We never sell personal information. Leads a brokerage gets from its own website or ads are never resold.

## How we use it

To provide the service the brokerage asked for: storing records, sending the messages the brokerage or agent chooses
to send, reminders, reports, AI help on the brokerage's own records, and keeping accounts secure.

- **Texts and calls** to clients are sent only with their consent and only between 8am and 9pm their time. Replying
  STOP always works.
- **AI** is given only the signed-in brokerage's records. AI suggestions are shown for a person to check before
  anything changes.
- **Recordings** of calls and appointments are made only with everyone's consent.

## Who else sees it

Only the brokerage the information belongs to. Inside a brokerage, an agent's personal contacts are private unless
the brokerage chooses otherwise. RightAlly support sees a brokerage's account only for a stated reason and for at most
60 minutes, and every visit is listed for the brokerage. The outside companies that help run the service are listed in
the [subprocessor list](subprocessors.md).

## How long we keep it

As long as the brokerage chooses (Settings → Privacy). Backups are kept for _[period, default 35 days, flagged for
review]_. Practice records are deleted after 14 days. Files sent for concierge setup are deleted after 30 days.

## Your choices

Anyone can ask to see, correct or delete their information, from any brokerage website ("Privacy requests") or by
asking the brokerage. Requests are confirmed by email and answered within 45 days.

## Security

Each brokerage's sensitive data is encrypted with its own key. Sign-in uses passkeys or a second step. Every change is
written to a tamper-evident audit log. Clients' personal details are kept out of system logs and error reports.
