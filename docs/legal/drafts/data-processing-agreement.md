# DRAFT: Data processing agreement (for attorney review)

_Outline in plain language. The attorney to turn into contract terms._

1. **Roles.** The brokerage is the business (controller); RightAlly is its service provider (processor) for the
   brokerage's data.
2. **Instructions.** RightAlly processes the brokerage's data only to provide the service the brokerage chose, as set
   in its settings, and as the law requires.
3. **No selling or sharing.** RightAlly does not sell or share the brokerage's data, or use it for anyone else.
   Brokerage leads are never resold. AI is not trained on brokerage data _(confirm with each AI provider's terms)_.
4. **Confidentiality.** RightAlly staff may see a brokerage's account only for support, with a stated reason, for at
   most 60 minutes. Every visit is listed for the brokerage, and the brokerage can require that staff ask first.
5. **Security.** As described in the security controls (HQ → Controls): separation between brokerages at the page,
   record and database level; per-brokerage encryption keys; two-step sign-in; audit log; logs without client details;
   a content security policy; dependency checks on every change; weekly AI red-team tests.
6. **Subprocessors.** As listed in the subprocessor list. New ones are notified _(default: 30 days ahead, flagged for
   review)_, and the brokerage can object.
7. **Help with requests.** Tools for the brokerage to answer access, correction and deletion requests (Settings →
   Privacy), and help from RightAlly support.
8. **Breaches.** RightAlly tells the brokerage without undue delay _(default: within 72 hours of confirming, flagged for
   review)_, with what is known and what is being done.
9. **End of service.** The brokerage can export its data at any time. After cancellation, data is deleted _(default:
   within 30 days, backups within 35 more days, flagged for review)_, unless the law requires keeping it.
10. **Audits.** SOC 2 report when available; before then, the controls page and answers to reasonable questionnaires.

## Questions for the attorney

2. Limits of liability and indemnity for data incidents.
3. Wording for AI use of brokerage data (no training; provider retention limits).
