# Delivery plan: Sprint 0 to version 1.0

Two-week sprints, **five teams** (about 22 people). Every sprint starts from a detailed specification (after an approved best-in-class review) and ends with green CI and a merged pull request.

Change log: Deal-IQ, DocIQ, List-IQ, AI Networking Assistant, compensation additions, Broker Risk Dashboard, appointment flow, Lead-IQ (AI follow-up and AI Prospector), Learn-IQ, broker-branded assistant and broker voice messages added; **fifth team approved** (Team E: growth and AI engagement) and work rebalanced. Version 1.0 at Sprint 58 (committed 62), previously 67 (committed 71) with four teams.

**Definition of ready (every module):** before a module's detailed specification is written, a best-in-class review of the leading products is done, the features that would most improve users' productivity, closings and efficiency are recommended with their timeline effect, and the owner approves them.

**Definition of done (screens):** every sprint that ships screens passes a usability test with five real agents or brokers against the easy-to-use principles (product/ux-principles.md).
