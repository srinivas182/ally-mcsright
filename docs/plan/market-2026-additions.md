## The additions

### 1. Daily brief across the whole business (Teams A, D and E)

- One ranked list each morning, for agents and for brokers: hot website chats, leads due a call (Today's 10),
  contract deadlines (Deal-IQ), compliance items to fix, money waiting to be paid out, and expiring licences.
- Each item says **why** it matters and gives the next step, done in one click with a preview first.
- Brokers get their own version: deals at risk, agents not following up, payouts waiting for approval, and credits
  running low.
- **Done when:** one screen and one daily email/text sum it up; each item links to its action; the brief is
  ranked by commission at stake; it works without AI (written rules), and AI improves the wording.

### 2. Ad results in commission dollars, with an automatic stop (Team A with E; Connect C4b)

- Every campaign shows ad spend, leads, appointments, closings, gross commission, and the **agent's take-home
  after the split**. The figures come from the ledger, not estimates.
- The agent sets a target cost per appointment. When the target is broken for 3 days, the campaign **pauses
  itself** and says why.
- **Done when:** results match the ledger to the cent, the pause works on both own-account ads and RightAlly
  Boost, and the agent is told every time it pauses.

### 3. Buyer demand before a home is listed (Teams C and E)

- Before the listing appointment, the agent enters an address and gets a branded **demand report**:
    - buyers in the brokerage's CRM and website whose saved searches, viewed homes and price band match;
    - how demand changes at each price point;
    - similar homes for sale.
- It shows counts only, never names, so it breaks no privacy rules. The listing agent can then contact the
  matching buyers' agents.
- **Done when:** the report is ready in under 10 seconds, the counts come from first-party data and never show
  personal details, and it reads well in English and Spanish.

### 4. Switch in a weekend, with two-way sync while moving (Team A; Connect C2, C5)

- Import from the old CRM and transaction system, then **keep two-way sync running** for up to 90 days so
  nothing is lost while the team moves.
- There is a "moving status" board: what has come across, what still needs attention, and who hasn't logged in
  yet.
- **Done when:** a test brokerage moves contacts, deals and documents from each supported app with no duplicates,
  and during sync a change made in either app appears in the other.

### 5. Proof of consent that travels with every contact (Teams D and A)

- Each contact records texting and calling consent: where it came from (form, chat, import, connected app),
  the exact wording, the date, and evidence such as a page snapshot or an email.
- Imported contacts arrive with consent "unknown" unless proof comes with them. Automated texts require proof.
- **Done when:** every automated text checks it, the broker can export a consent report for any contact, and
  an opt-out in a connected app stops texts here.

### 6. Fair Housing checks on every channel, with an audit pack (Team D)

- One check for ads, texts, AIlly, websites, AI content and listing descriptions. What it changed is logged by
  channel.
- A **monthly compliance pack** goes to the broker as a PDF to show regulators or the MLS.
- **Done when:** every outbound AI text and ad passes through the check, and the pack lists what was changed and
  why.

### 7. Agent Wallet (Team B)

- One screen showing: earnings this year, progress to cap, deals pending and the expected pay, revenue share
  earned and pending, "get paid now" limits, and money owed.
- Agents see it in the mobile web app, with a monthly statement.
- **Done when:** every figure comes from the ledger, ties to the statements, and refreshes as deals move.

### 8. Seller-funded listing boost (Team A with E; Connect C4b)

- The listing agent offers a boost and the seller pays by card. The boost is paid through RightAlly credits at
  HQ's margin.
- The seller gets a weekly branded report: views, clicks, leads and showings.
- **Done when:** the seller can pay without an account, unused budget is refunded when the boost stops, and the
  report goes out on schedule.
