# AI 04 — List-IQ: the listing studio (Listing Marketing Centre; Team C, Sprints 55–57)

Status: **Module definition approved.** Detailed specification after its best-in-class review
(definition of ready).

## Purpose

The agent takes photos on their phone at the property; List-IQ turns them into professional
photos, motion videos and a high-converting listing draft, ready to publish. Saves the agent hours
and the cost of a photographer for most listings. Upgrades the Listing Marketing Centre (AI
descriptions, video and 360° tours, floor plans, open-house sign-in).

## How it works

1. **Guided shoot:** a room-by-room shot list with on-screen framing guides.
2. **Professional photos:** light and colour balanced, windows recovered, lines straightened, skies
   improved; optional **virtual staging and decluttering**, labelled "virtually staged" as MLSs
   require.
3. **Motion videos:** a property video with movement and licensed music, plus vertical reels for
   Instagram, Facebook and TikTok; optional AI voice-over (English and Spanish).
4. **Listing draft:** headline, description and key features within MLS character limits, checked
   for fair-housing language; MLS fields pre-filled from the photos and public record.
5. **Publish in one go:** property website, social posts, flyer and email blast; a ready-to-paste
   MLS input sheet, or direct submission where an MLS offers an approved listing-input connection.
6. **Floor plans** from a phone scan on LiDAR-equipped phones.

## Guardrails

- Edits never hide or remove permanent defects (cracks, damage, power lines, views).
- Virtually staged or altered images are labelled as MLS rules require.
- Every output passes AI Ad & Flyer Compliance; the agent approves before anything is published.
