# Know 01 — Learn-IQ: the agent academy (Team E)

Status: **Module definition approved** (upgrade of Training & Knowledge Base).

## Purpose

Take every agent from new licensee to top producer, step by step, with an AI instructor.

## Capabilities

1. **Learning paths:** new agent onboarding, buyer side, listing side, contracts and compliance,
   lead conversion, business planning; brokerages add their own paths and policies.
2. **Short videos and guides** (brokerage uploads plus RightAlly library), with transcripts.
3. **AI instructor:** ask questions by text or voice at any point; answers from the lesson, the
   brokerage's policies and Compliance Knowledge.
4. **Practice on a sandbox deal:** write a contract with Deal-IQ drafting, find the errors planted
   in a sample transaction, respond to an inspection report, all scored with feedback.
5. **Voice role-play:** the AI plays a seller with objections, a nervous buyer or a listing
   appointment; scored on the brokerage's talk tracks.
6. **Pass to unlock:** quizzes and practice must pass before the next module; certificates.
7. **Broker dashboard:** progress by agent and team, stuck points, and links to production.
8. Official state continuing-education credit through an approved partner provider.

## New Agent Launch Program (positioning)

Most brokerages onboard new agents who know little. RightAlly includes the training: **"Your new
agents learn, close sooner and stay compliant, without hiring a trainer."**

- **First 90 days, guided:** onboarding path assigned on invitation; daily drills; mentor match;
  weekly AI coach check-in; first-deal checklist.
- **Extra guardrails for new agents:** Deal-IQ drafts need broker approval until the agent is
  certified; every file is checked by the compliance scan before it reaches the broker.
- **Broker view:** each new agent's progress, readiness and first-deal status on one screen.

Wording guardrail: say "every file checked before it reaches you", not "100% compliant"; the broker
remains responsible for supervision.
