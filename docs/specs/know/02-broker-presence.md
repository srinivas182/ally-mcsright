# Know 02 — Broker presence: branded AI assistant and voice recognition (Team E)

Status: **Module definition approved** (part of the RightAlly AI Assistant and Agent Activity &
Engagement).

## Broker-branded AI assistant

Agents see the assistant with **the broker's photo and name** (e.g. "Sarah's AI assistant") and
can text or speak to it. It answers from the brokerage's policies, training and Compliance
Knowledge, and hands anything it cannot answer to the real broker or staff. Always labelled as AI.

## Voice recognition messages

On milestones (joined, first deal, deal closed, capped, anniversary, recruit made) the agent gets a
short personal voice message in the broker's voice, written for that moment.

Rules: the broker gives **written consent** for voice cloning (revocable; the voice model is
deleted on withdrawal); used **only for recognition messages**; every message is marked
"AI voice message from Sarah"; it can **never** carry instructions, payments or wire details;
brokers may record real messages instead.
