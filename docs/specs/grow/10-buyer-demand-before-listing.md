# Grow 10 — Buyer demand before a home is listed

Sprint BD1. Teams C with E. Addition 3 in `docs/plan/market-2026-additions.md`; answers Compass's "buyer demand
at each price point" with the brokerage's own first-party data.

## The job

Before the listing appointment the agent enters the address, ZIP, expected price and the home's basics (beds,
baths, size) under **Grow → Buyer demand**, and gets in a few seconds:

- **How many buyers want a home like this**: contacts whose active saved search would send them this home (place
  by ZIP, city or map area; price, bedrooms, bathrooms and size within their limits), plus buyer leads from the
  last 12 months for this ZIP whose price range includes the price. Each person counted once.
- **Demand by price**: the same count at 85% to 115% of the price, in 5% steps, so the agent can show the seller
  where demand drops off.
- **Website shoppers**: how many visitors looked at homes in this ZIP within 15% of the price in the last 90 days,
  and how many saved one.
- **Agents with matching buyers**: the brokerage's agents who hold those buyers (the saved search's agent, else
  the contact's owner) and how many each. "Tell these agents it's coming" emails each of them once (counts only;
  they know their own buyers) with the listing agent's name and email, so they can ask for a preview.
- **The competition**: similar homes for sale now (same ZIP, within 15% of the price), the count, the median
  asking price and up to six with MLS attribution.

## Privacy

Counts only. The report, the page and the printout never show a buyer's name or contact details; agents' names
are shown because they are colleagues. All data is the brokerage's own (CRM, website) or licensed MLS data.

## The report for the seller

"Report for the seller" opens a branded, printable page (brokerage name, agent, date), in English or Spanish
("En español"), with the headline number, the price curve, website shoppers and the competition, the counts-only
note and Equal Housing Opportunity. Not indexed by search engines; only the agent who ran it, owners and staff.

## Acceptance (BD1)

- Matching follows saved-search rules (place, price, beds, baths, size) and buyer leads; each buyer counted once.
- The curve, agents, shoppers and competition are right; nothing personal leaves the CRM.
- The printout reads in English and Spanish; telling agents happens once; the report is ready in under 10 seconds
  (one pass over saved searches and leads, a handful of indexed queries).
