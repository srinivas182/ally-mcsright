# Grow 02 — Lead-IQ: leads the brokerage owns, worked by AI (Team E)

Status: **Module definition approved** (upgrade of the Lead Generation Engine with instant lead
response, scoring and distribution). Detailed specifications follow their best-in-class reviews.

## Why

Agents pay portals and referral networks 30–50% of their commission for leads, and most leads
die from slow or inconsistent follow-up. Lead-IQ generates leads the brokerage owns, works every
one by AI text and voice until it is ready, and hands the agent a qualified lead with a summary.
Benchmarks: Ylopo (90-day AI follow-up, calls within minutes) and Structurely (12-month AI ISA
nurture); both are add-ons to other CRMs, while Lead-IQ runs inside RightAlly's CRM, Deal-IQ and
compensation.

No scraping of people's personal data; licensed data and first-party signals only.

## 2. AI follow-up that never stops

- **Speed to lead:** every new lead gets an AI text or call **within one minute**, around the clock.
- **AI inside sales agent (text and voice):** natural conversations that answer questions from the
  listing data and the brokerage's knowledge, and **qualify**: buying or selling, timeline, area,
  price range, pre-approval, home to sell first, motivation, whether they already have an agent.
- **Personalised from history:** each message or call uses everything known (homes viewed, saved
  searches, valuation, past conversations, market changes), e.g. "Two new homes matching your
  search came up in Fondren this week."
- **Long nurture:** smart cadence over 12 months (not a fixed drip), backing off or speeding up by
  engagement; new listings, price drops and market updates as reasons to reach out.
- **Booking:** showings and listing appointments booked straight onto the agent's calendar.
- **Warm transfer:** a hot lead on an AI call can be transferred live to the agent's phone.

## 3. Hand-off the agent loves

When a lead is qualified, the agent gets a push notification and a one-screen **lead brief**:
who they are, what they want (budget, area, timeline, pre-approval), why now, homes they viewed,
the conversation summary with the recording and transcript, suggested next step and a **drafted
first message** in the agent's voice. One tap to accept, call or reply. If the agent doesn't
accept within the offer window, lead distribution offers it to the next agent (brokerage rules).

## 4. Results

Lead ROI by source down to closings and commission; response times; conversion by agent;
the cost compared with portal referral fees, so the broker sees what Lead-IQ saves.

## Compliance (built in, not optional)

- **Consent:** AI voice calls and automated texts only to leads who gave consent on a form with
  TCPA consent wording (the FCC treats AI-generated voices as artificial voices under the TCPA);
  consent records kept per lead.
- **Do Not Call** scrubbing, calling hours by the lead's time zone, state rules (e.g. state
  telemarketing laws), opt-out by "STOP" or by saying so on a call, honoured everywhere at once.
- **AI disclosure:** the AI says it is an AI assistant for the agent or brokerage.
- **Call recording consent** announced where state law requires all-party consent.
- **A2P 10DLC** registration for texting; Fair Housing guardrails on every message.
