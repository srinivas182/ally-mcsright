# Ready-made library (SU3)

Status: Done. Tracker: A21 Done (SU1, SU2 and SU3).

## What it does, in plain words

**Settings → Ready-made library** (owners and staff).

- **Commission plans:**
    - 80/20 with an $18,000 cap and a $395 fee per deal;
    - 70/30 with a $16,000 cap, for new agents;
    - 90/10 with no cap;
    - a flat $395 per deal.

    They are always installed as **drafts**. The owner checks and publishes them, and nothing is paid until then.

- **Follow-up plans:**
    - an investor lead (30 days);
    - a renter thinking about buying (90 days);
    - someone moving here from out of town (21 days);
    - a new seller in Spanish (14 days).

    They are added to the everyday plans every brokerage already has, and shared with the whole brokerage.

- **Message templates**, shared with the whole brokerage:
    - showing confirmed;
    - offer sent;
    - under contract;
    - closing day;
    - ask for a review;
    - price drop;
    - Spanish versions of showing confirmed and under contract.
- Each item shows what it is, a **What's in it** preview, and one **Install** button.
- An **English / Español** filter.
- **Updates without surprises.** Every install remembers a fingerprint of what was put in.
    - When RightAlly improves an item, a copy nobody changed shows **New version available**, and one click updates it.
    - A copy someone changed shows **You changed your copy** and is **never overwritten**. They can install the new version alongside it as a new copy.
    - Commission plans are never updated in place: a newer version arrives as a new draft.
- Every item passes the same Fair Housing screen and merge-field check as anything a person writes. A test installs the whole library to prove it.
