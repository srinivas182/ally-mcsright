# Learn 03: The AI role-play academy (LN3)

Status: built in LN3. Tracker row E20, part 2 of 2. Module: `modules/Learn` (People › Learn › Practice).

## What the market leaders do

- **Role-play tools**
    - **Tom Ferry**, **Buffini** and **ICC** run live role-play calls with a coach at fixed times.
    - **Second Nature**, **Yoodli** and **Hyperbound** offer AI role-play for sales teams in general. They are not
      built around real estate conversations or US real estate rules, and they sit outside the agent's tools.
- **Brokerage platforms** have no practice space. Agents practise on real clients.
- **Where they fall short:**
    - **Hard to fit in.** Coaching is expensive and needs a schedule.
    - **Practice is not private.** Agents aged 50+ do not want to practise in front of colleagues, and AI
      scorecards are often shared league tables.
    - **Uneven marking.** Feedback is graded differently each time.

## What RightAlly does

### Practise a conversation

There are eight real estate scenarios, each with a goal:

- a seller who wants a lower commission;
- an expired listing;
- a for-sale-by-owner;
- a nervous first-time buyer;
- a buyer who will not sign the buyer agreement;
- an open house visitor;
- asking for a price reduction;
- an upset buyer after the inspection.

**How it runs**

- The AI plays a **made-up** client, in character, for up to 16 turns.
- **Type or speak.** "Speak and listen" uses the browser's own speech recognition and voice. Only the words are
  sent, never audio.

**Feedback**

- **The same checklist for everyone,** set in code:
    - listened;
    - asked good questions;
    - handled the concern;
    - agreed a next step;
    - stayed within the rules (no promises, no fair housing issues, no legal advice).
- **The score is counted in code**, from the items met. Items the AI makes up are ignored.
- **Coaching:** up to three points that went well, up to three to try next time, and one better line to say.

### Today's five-minute drill

- One scenario a day per person; the same all day, different the next day.
- A streak counts days in a row.

### Rehearse a real appointment

- The agent describes the appointment in a sentence or two. No names are needed.
- The AI plays that client.

### Coaching cards

**From notes on a call just made**, or **a deal just closed**, the agent gets:

- what went well;
- what to try next time;
- the single most useful next step in the next 48 hours;
- a one-line lesson.

### Top-producer playbook

A lesson from a deal debrief can be **suggested for the office playbook**. It becomes a draft knowledge base page
in "Playbooks". Only the broker can publish it.

### Scorecard

The skill scorecard (LN2) now shows each skill's average practice score next to the quiz average.

## Privacy and safety

- **Encrypted and private.** Everything typed in practice is encrypted at rest and visible **only to the agent**,
  not the broker. The broker sees, for the last 30 days, only how many sessions each person did and their average
  checklist score.
- **AI safety.** The AI client never uses real names or personal details and never gives legal or tax advice.
  Notes go to AI as untrusted content.
- **Credits.** 1 per client reply, 2 per feedback, 2 per coaching card.
